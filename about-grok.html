About this shop and this guide site

Makerly Paper Co. is a small shop that makes planners, personal finance spreadsheets, and printables. The main readers are in the United States, so files ship as US Letter first, then A4.

Planners are printable PDFs, including dated year pages and, when it helps, linked PDFs for tablet apps. Personal finance tools are Excel workbooks that also work in Google Sheets, often with a one-page printable. Other printables are paper-first PDFs such as inserts and trackers.

This site is a set of how-to guides from the Grok side of the shop. It sits at a separate address from the main Makerly blog so the two sites can publish different articles and still point to the same shop.

When a guide links to a template, Makerly Paper Co. made that template, and the page says so. Links to the shop use tracking codes so we can see which guide sent a visit. That is our own shop, not an outside affiliate program.
